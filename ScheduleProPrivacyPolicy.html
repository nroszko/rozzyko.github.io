Privacy Policy

SPCC Concur Expense Automation — Last updated: September 12, 2026

This extension is a private, internal browser extension built for use within a single organization. It is not distributed publicly and is only intended for authorized internal users on that organization's own systems. This policy explains what data the extension accesses, why, and what happens to it.

What the extension does

The extension automates a single internal workflow: importing the list of employees scheduled to work a given shift from an internal scheduling system into an expense-reporting form, so users don't have to type in attendees by hand.

What data it accesses

  • An existing session identifier. When you're logged in to the organization's expense-reporting system, the extension reads a session identifier already present on that page. This is used only to verify, via an internal automation workflow, that you are an authorized user and to obtain short-lived credentials for the scheduling system on your behalf.
  • Scheduling/roster data. The extension calls the organization's internal scheduling system to retrieve employee names, employee numbers, and scheduled shifts for a date/shift you select, in order to match and pre-fill attendees.
  • Form field values you've already entered. The extension reads values you have entered on the expense form (such as the date and shift type) to know what roster to fetch, and writes attendee selections / email addresses back into that form.

Where data goes

  • Your session identifier is sent, over HTTPS, to an internal automation workflow operated by the organization, solely to confirm you are on the authorized user list and to issue scoped, time-limited credentials for the scheduling system. It is not sent anywhere else and is not used for any other purpose.
  • Roster requests are sent, over HTTPS, directly to the organization's internal scheduling system using those credentials.
  • No data is sent to the extension developer, to any analytics or advertising service, or to any party outside of the organization's own internal systems described above.

What's stored, and for how long

The extension uses the browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local), which stays on your own device and is not synced or transmitted elsewhere. It stores:

  • Your session identifier and a cache of temporary scheduling-system credentials, for up to 1 hour, so you don't have to re-authenticate on every request.
  • A cache of the employee roster list, for up to 12 hours, to speed up repeated lookups.

All of this data is cleared automatically whenever the browser restarts or the extension is installed/updated, and can be cleared manually at any time from the extension's popup.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not track browsing activity outside the specific internal pages it operates on.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising identifiers.
  • It does not sell or share data with third parties.
  • It does not run any code that isn't packaged in the extension itself (no remotely loaded scripts).

Who this applies to

This extension is built for, and restricted to, authorized employees of the organization it was built for, with access to that organization's own internal systems. It is not intended for use by the general public and is not available outside that organization.

Changes to this policy

If how the extension handles data changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.

Contact

Questions about this extension or this policy can be directed to the internal team that maintains it.
