Privacy Policy for SchedulePro Enhancements

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Publisher: Rozzyko (Nathan Roszko)

This policy explains how SchedulePro Enhancements, an extension for Microsoft Edge, accesses, uses, stores, and transmits information. It covers the extension's interface enhancements, scheduling tools, import and export tools, and Schedule Template Builder.

Information the extension accesses

The extension accesses information available through your existing SchedulePro session at app1.scheduleproweb.com and files you choose to open in its tools. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Employee names, employee numbers, and internal employee identifiers.
  • Shift dates, shift codes, working assignments, teams, crews, groups, and leader selections.
  • Vacation, sick-leave, and other absence codes included in schedules. These codes may reveal leave-related or health-related information.
  • Schedule data and supporting fields contained in selected CSV or Excel files and SchedulePro exports.
  • Extension preferences, installation and update metadata, and diagnostic information such as errors, timestamps, and relevant SchedulePro page URLs.

The extension does not require a separate account or ask you to provide your SchedulePro password. Requests to SchedulePro use your existing signed-in browser session.

How information is used

Information is used to display and format schedules, select employees and dates, populate scheduling forms, apply group filters, identify consecutive sick shifts, generate formatted workbooks, compare proposed schedule changes with existing schedules, prepare import files, and build schedule templates. Employee identifiers are also used to support employee and leader filtering tools.

When you use formatted exports, the extension may retrieve employee team-code data from SchedulePro automatically to populate crew assignments. Import comparison tools may retrieve a current schedule export to identify changes or conflicts.

Processing and transmission

File conversion, workbook generation, schedule comparison, and template building run in your browser. The extension communicates with SchedulePro over HTTPS to retrieve scheduling data and support the filtering, editing, and import actions you use. Preparing a workbook in the Schedule Template Builder does not submit it to SchedulePro. The import tool can prepare a CSV and place it in SchedulePro's upload control; you complete the import using the SchedulePro page.

The extension does not send employee records, schedules, selected files, or diagnostic logs to servers operated by Rozzyko. It does not include advertising, tracking services, or external analytics, and Rozzyko does not sell or rent information accessed by the extension.

Some preferences use Microsoft Edge's extension sync storage. When browser synchronization is enabled and available, Microsoft Edge may synchronize those settings through Microsoft's services. Employee-to-crew caches use local extension storage rather than sync storage.

Storage and retention

  • Local extension storage: The extension saves employee-number-to-crew mappings, cache timestamps, selected group and leader identifiers, and export sheet selections. These records persist across browser sessions until overwritten or cleared; the crew cache has no automatic expiry.
  • Browser sync storage: Feature preferences, debugging options, and installation or update metadata remain until changed or removed through the browser's storage and synchronization controls.
  • Temporary processing: Open pages hold working schedule data and selected file contents in memory. Pending sick-shift warning information, including an employee name and dates, may be held in browser session storage. Closing the relevant pages releases their working data; session storage is cleared when the browser session ends.
  • Diagnostics: The extension writes diagnostic messages to the browser console, some of which may contain employee identifiers, names, dates, or shift codes. Some messages appear even when debug mode is off. The background process keeps up to 100 recent error reports in memory. These logs are not automatically uploaded to Rozzyko.
  • Files and clipboard: Files you download remain wherever you save them until you delete them. Generated workbooks can contain hidden sheets with original export records and metadata needed for later import comparisons, including records beyond those displayed on selected visible sheets. If you use the identifier tool's copy function, its results are placed on your clipboard.

Your choices and access to information

You can change available feature preferences in the extension's options, choose which files to open, and review generated files before importing or sharing them. You can stop the extension's access by disabling or removing it through Microsoft Edge's Manage extensions page. Reload or close affected SchedulePro pages after doing so.

Removing the extension removes its local extension storage. Use Microsoft Edge's synchronization controls to manage synchronized preferences. Resetting feature preferences alone does not clear cached employee data. Disabling the extension does not delete its stored data, and removing it does not delete downloaded files, clipboard copies, or records already held by SchedulePro. Delete exported files and clear clipboard history separately when needed.

You can view and correct scheduling records through SchedulePro to the extent permitted by your account. For access, correction, or deletion of employment and scheduling records held by your organization or SchedulePro, contact your organization's SchedulePro administrator. Rozzyko does not receive those records through the extension and cannot modify or delete them remotely.

Other services

SchedulePro, your organization, and Microsoft process information under their own policies and arrangements. Files saved to a synchronized folder or copied through a synchronized clipboard may also be processed by the services you use. This policy describes the extension's behavior and does not replace those services' privacy policies.
